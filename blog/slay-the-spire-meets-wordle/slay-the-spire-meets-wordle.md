# Slay the Spire Meets Wordle: A New Daily Card Puzzle Is Here

If you like *Slay the Spire* for its tight card mechanics, or *Wordle* for its daily brain-sparking challenge, there’s a weird little game that sits perfectly in between: **Lethal**.

In *Lethal*, you only get one move to win a battle. You're given a set of cards. You know the enemy's health. Your job is to figure out the exact combo that deals *precisely enough damage* to win. Nothing more, nothing less.

It’s quick. It’s brutal. And it’s way more satisfying than it should be.

### What Makes It Work

- **One-turn puzzles** – Like chess problems, but with cards.
- **No randomness** – Every puzzle is hand-built and fully solvable.
- **Free, no download** – Just open your browser and start playing.

You get a new challenge every day, and solving it feels like cracking a code. You either see the logic... or you don’t (until it clicks and you feel brilliant).
