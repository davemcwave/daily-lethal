Card Wording Style Guide

Daily Lethal - internal reference for card descriptions

Rules

1 HP - always uppercase. ✗ health, hp, Health   ✓ HP
2 Gain X HP - never "Heal". ✗ Heal 1 HP   ✓ Gain 1 HP
3 Imperative verbs - no third-person. ✗ Deals damage. Inflicts Vulnerable. Consumes energy.
✓ Deal damage. Inflict Vulnerable. Consume energy.
4 Periods - every sentence ends with one.
5 Periods between clauses - never commas. ✗ Deal 2 damage, Gain 1 energy   ✓ Deal 2 damage. Gain 1 energy.
6 No + prefix on gains. ✗ Gain +2 energy   ✓ Gain 2 energy
7 Always include "Your" before "next X attacks/cards". ✗ Next 2 attacks have Flame   ✓ Your next 2 attacks have Flame
8 Always "your discard pile" - never "the discard" or "discard". ✗ if discard is empty   ✓ if your discard pile is empty
9 "less than X" - never "below X". ✗ If below 3 health   ✓ If you have less than 3 HP
10 "Inflict" for debuffs on the enemy - never "Apply". ✗ Apply Vulnerable   ✓ Inflict Vulnerable
11 No parentheses. ✗ Remove all (your) status effects.   ✓ Remove all your status effects.
12 Numerals not words. ✗ Trash up to two cards   ✓ Trash up to 2 cards
13 "action" not "effect" when referring to what a card does - avoids confusion with status effects. ✗ Repeat the effect of the next card you play.   ✓ Repeat the action of the next card you play.

BBcode Colors

Damage dealt / HP lost [color=red]X[/color]
HP gained [color=#008c10]X[/color]
All keywords (Vulnerable, Burn, Corrode, Sharp, Critical, Flame, Poison, Charge) [color=#e67e22]Keyword[/color]
✓ Color a number only if it is the primary effect output - the thing being dealt, gained, or lost.
✓ "Deal 2 damage." — 2 is the damage output
✓ "Gain 1 HP." — 1 is the HP gain
✓ "Lose 2 HP." — 2 is the HP loss
✗ Do not color thresholds, target states, counts, or conditional values.
✗ "Reduce your HP to 1." — 1 is a target state, not an effect amount
✗ "Deal damage 3 times." — 3 is a repeat count
✗ "If you have 0 energy." — 0 is a condition
✗ "The next 3 times you play a card." — 3 is a duration count
✗ "Top 2 cards / next 2 cards." — 2 is a card count

Keywords Glossary

🗡 Sharp — player buff
Your attacks deal X more damage, where X is your Sharp stacks. Lose 1 Sharp per card played.
⚡ Critical — player buff
Your next attack deals double damage. Consumed on use.
🔥 Burn — enemy debuff
Each card played deals damage equal to the Burn stacks, then reduces Burn by 1.
💀 Vulnerable — enemy debuff
Enemy takes increased damage. Applied by Venom, Berserk, Haunt, Wound.
🧪 Corrode — enemy debuff
Applied by CorrodeCard.
☁ Charge — attack modifier
The attack gains energy instead of dealing damage.
🔥 Flame — attack modifier
The attack inflicts Burn instead of dealing damage.
☠ Poison — attack modifier
The attack inflicts Vulnerable instead of dealing damage.
