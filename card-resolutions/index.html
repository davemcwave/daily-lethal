Card order

Every card you play goes through these steps.

  1. Pay costReverberate costs 3 energy
  2. Apply card effectsTop to bottom, like Reverberate's
  3. Player statuses react to the cardRegen heals 1
  4. Enemy statuses react to the cardBurn deals 15
  5. Discard cardReverberate goes to the discard pile
  6. Check if you won or lostYou win when the enemy's HP hits 0

Attack Order

Pound: 4, +2 (Sharp) = 6, x2 (Critical) = 12, split into 12 hits of 1, +1 each (Vulnerable) = 24 damage. Lifesteal heals 24. The card shows the result: 24.

Attack order

How an attack card's damage is worked out.

  1. Base damagePound deals 4. The Rage card increases base damage.
  2. AddSharp 2: +2 to Pound's damage
  3. MultiplyCritical: × 2 to Pound's damage
  4. SplitSplinter: 12 hits of 1
  5. ConvertCharge, Flame, Poison (not shown: they replace the damage)
  6. Deal damageVulnerable 1: +1 to each hit
  7. ReactionsLifesteal heals 24, Retaliate hits back

"Your next attack" means your next attack card. Critical, Poison, Splinter and the like apply to every hit of that card and are used up once. A card replayed by Echo or Reverberate is a new attack.

Want to see the math? Turn on Options → Show Damage Math to see it beside the card.
